OUR STORY

Powering Pakistan Since 1986

Power Tech Generators was founded in 1986 by Latif Ahmed with a simple mission: to provide reliable power solutions to Pakistan's growing industries.

What started as a small generator repair workshop in Karachi has now grown into Pakistan's most trusted diesel generator company with 40+ years of excellence.

Over the past 40+ years, we have successfully completed over 10,000 projects and served more than 25,000 happy customers across Pakistan.
