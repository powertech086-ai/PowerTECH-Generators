About Power Tech

Pakistan's most trusted diesel generator company with 40+ years of excellence in power solutions.

OUR STORY

Powering Pakistan Since 1986

Power Tech Generators was founded in 1986 with a simple mission: to provide reliable power solutions to Pakistan's growing industries. What started as a small generator repair workshop in Karachi has now grown into Pakistan's most trusted diesel generator company.

Over the past 40+ years, we have successfully completed over 10,000 projects and served more than 25,000 happy customers across Pakistan. From textile mills to hospitals, banks to data centers, construction sites to wedding halls – we keep Pakistan running.

We are the authorized dealer of world's leading generator brands including Caterpillar, Cummins, Perkins, FG Wilson, Denyo, and John Deere. Our state-of-the-art workshop in Karachi is equipped with the latest diagnostic and repair tools.
